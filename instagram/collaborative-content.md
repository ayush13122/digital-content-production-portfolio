# Collaborative Instagram Content

## Overview
Document selected collaborative reels and video-editing work.

## Case Study Template
For each reel, record:
- Public post URL
- Date published
- Content format and objective
- Your exact contribution (editing, cuts, pacing, transitions, captions, effects, or publishing)
- Tools used
- Permission and collaborator credit status
- Verified post metrics with capture date

## Attribution
Clearly distinguish your contribution from the work of other account owners or collaborators. Do not claim ownership of another creator's footage or account-wide performance.
