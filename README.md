# Digital Content Production Portfolio
## AI-Assisted Social Media & Video Production

**Status: Documentation In Progress**

This portfolio documents selected digital-media work across Instagram and YouTube, including AI-assisted content ideation, scriptwriting, Google Flow video generation, editing, and collaborative creator work.

## Portfolio sections

### 1. Techbyte AI — Instagram
- Short-form technology and comedy content.
- Ideas, concepts, scripts, and prompts developed with Gemini and ChatGPT.
- AI video assets generated using Google Flow.
- Selected post links, examples, and verified performance metrics will be documented.

### 2. Collaborative Instagram content
- Selected short-form content and video-editing contributions.
- Each case study will state the contributor's exact role.
- Views and engagement figures will be linked to the specific post and capture date.

### 3. FunTech — YouTube
- Technology and gaming content.
- Laptop/product review videos and Shorts.
- Selected video links, thumbnails, and production notes will be documented.

### 4. Affiliate marketing exploration
- Amazon Associates account setup and dashboard exploration.
- Current activity and metrics will be recorded accurately.
- Account setup alone will not be represented as a completed campaign, sale, or conversion.

## Production workflow
1. Research and content idea development
2. Scriptwriting and prompt preparation
3. AI-assisted visual/video generation where appropriate
4. Editing, pacing, captions, and platform-specific formatting
5. Publishing and performance documentation

## Evidence and attribution
Include only content that can be publicly linked or shared with permission. Clearly distinguish personal work from collaborative work. Do not imply ownership of another creator's footage, account, or performance metrics. Redact private account information from screenshots.
